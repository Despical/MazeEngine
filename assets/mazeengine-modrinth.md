<h1><strong>Maze Engine</strong></h1><br>
<h3><strong>Give players a place worth getting lost in.</strong></h3><br>
<br>
<strong>30 themed presets  ·  Private previews  ·  Personal route guides</strong><br>
Generate a maze, walk through your idea, and make it part of your world.<br>
<br>
<a href="https://docs.despical.dev/maze-engine/"><strong>Explore the documentation</strong></a>  ·  <a href="https://docs.despical.dev/maze-engine/preset-gallery/"><strong>See all 30 themes</strong></a></div><br>
<br>
Turn a corner of your hub into a garden labyrinth. Give an adventure area its own winding dungeon. Build a new course for your next community challenge. <strong>Maze Engine creates the structure and gives you the tools to shape, explore and maintain it.</strong><br>
<br>
Choose a theme and size, inspect a private preview, then build when it feels right. Your maze stays saved, its seed can be reused, and a personal arrow guide can lead you to the exit.<br>
<br>

<strong>Make a preset your own.</strong> Edit its YAML file to change weighted block palettes, floor materials, wall caps and inlays, roofs, lighting and decorations. Set passage width, wall thickness and wall height independently. Keep a bundled look or create a theme that fits your server.<br>

<hr><div align="center"><span><span><strong>02  /  TRY THE IDEA</strong></span></span><br>
<h2><strong>See your maze before it becomes your world.</strong></h2></div><br>
<br>
A private preview shows the planned maze using block displays. <strong>Only you see it, and your terrain stays untouched.</strong> Move around it, check the footprint, and inspect the paths and materials before committing to the build.<br>
<br>
<div align="center"><img src="https://docs.despical.dev/images/mazeengine/gameplay/private-preview.webp" alt="A private Hedge maze preview made of block displays, with the original terrain untouched."><br>
<span>A private preview viewed from above. Preview displays do not replace world blocks.</span><br>
<br>
<img src="https://docs.despical.dev/images/mazeengine/chat/chat-preview-hedge.png" alt="Hedge preview details with Create and Close chat buttons."><br>
<span>Review the dimensions and theme, then choose Create or Close.</span></div><br>
<br>
When you are ready, step outside the maze&#x27;s footprint and click <strong>Create</strong>. Maze Engine uses the preview&#x27;s retained seed, theme, dimensions and origin. <strong>Close</strong> dismisses the preview and removes its displays.<br>
<br>
<hr><div align="center"><span><span><strong>03  /  EXPLORE THE PATHS</strong></span></span><br>
<h2><strong>A way out, whenever you need one.</strong></h2></div><br>
<br>
A good maze invites exploration. When someone needs a hand, <strong>/maze solve &lt;name&gt;</strong> draws personal arrows toward the exit. The guide follows the shortest route from their current cell and adapts as they move.<br>
<br>
<div align="center"><img src="https://docs.despical.dev/images/mazeengine/gameplay/route-guide.webp" alt="Personal route arrows pointing through a Hedge maze towards its exit."><br>
<span>Personal route arrows inside a Hedge maze. Other players do not see your guide.</span></div><br>
<br>
The arrows sit above the floor without changing blocks. Customize their material and color, size and height, look-ahead distance and lifetime. Permission controls let you decide who can use the guide.<br>
<br>
<hr><div align="center"><span><span><strong>04  /  SHAPE THE CHALLENGE</strong></span></span><br>
<h2><strong>Keep the look. Change the journey.</strong></h2></div><br>
<br>
<strong>Revisit a favorite layout.</strong> Use the same seed, dimensions and preset settings to reproduce it. Share a seed with your builders, or regenerate a saved maze with a new seed for a fresh route.<br>
<br>
<strong>Choose how paths connect.</strong> PERFECT generation gives every pair of cells one connecting route. BRAIDED generation adds loops and alternative routes. Tune complexity separately from the maze&#x27;s materials and physical dimensions.<br>
<br>
<strong>Fit the space you have.</strong> Choose a cell grid, adjust passage and wall sizes, and place it at your location or explicit world coordinates. With WorldEdit or FAWE, fit a centered maze into a cuboid selection.<br>
<br>
<details><summary><strong>Seed and complexity explained in game</strong></summary><br>
<div align="center"><img src="https://docs.despical.dev/images/mazeengine/chat/chat-concepts-seed-complexity.png" alt="In-game help explaining deterministic seeds and maze complexity."></div><br>
Built-in help explains the controls with practical examples. Dimensions are logical cells; the physical footprint depends on passage width and wall thickness.<br>
<a href="https://docs.despical.dev/maze-engine/generation/">Read the generation and geometry guide</a><br>
</details><br>

<hr><div align="center"><span><span><strong>05  /  KEEP IT UNDER CONTROL</strong></span></span><br>
<h2><strong>Built once. Ready to manage.</strong></h2></div><br>
<br>
Saved layouts, ownership and teleport destinations remain available across server restarts. Browse your mazes with <strong>/maze list</strong> and open a details panel to see a maze&#x27;s theme, dimensions, seed, route length and status.<br>
<br>
<div align="center"><img src="https://docs.despical.dev/images/mazeengine/chat/chat-maze-list.png" alt="A list of saved mazes with themes, dimensions and ready status."><br>
<span>Find a saved maze and click through to its controls.</span><br>
<br>
<img src="https://docs.despical.dev/images/mazeengine/chat/chat-maze-details-hedge.png" alt="Hedge maze details with Teleport, Guide, Repair, Copy seed and Delete controls."><br>
<span>Teleport, Guide, Repair, Copy seed and Delete in one panel.</span></div><br>
<br>
Save a custom teleport point with <strong>/maze setspawn</strong>. Repair damaged structural blocks while keeping the saved layout, or regenerate the maze with another seed. Action permissions and ownership checks control who can manage each maze.<br>
<br>
<strong>Plan how terrain is handled.</strong> SAFE, REPLACE and CLEAR placement modes offer different rules for existing blocks. Enable an original-terrain snapshot with WorldEdit or FAWE when you want to restore the captured area after removing the maze.<br>
<br>
<strong>Build within your server&#x27;s budget.</strong> Block and time budgets spread world operations across ticks. Configurable cell, volume, chunk and concurrency limits keep the allowed workload in your hands. Saved operation state helps identify interrupted work that needs recovery.<br>
<br>
<strong>Keep the structure intact.</strong> Built-in protection covers player edits and common terrain changes. Mob spawning above maze footprints is blocked by default. Messages, chat buttons, hover text, visual settings and progress bars are configurable.<br>
<br>
<details><summary><strong>Repair help and the removal review</strong></summary><br>
<div align="center"><img src="https://docs.despical.dev/images/mazeengine/chat/chat-help-regenerate-hover.png" alt="Hover help explaining repair and regeneration."><br>
<span>Hover help explains the difference between rebuilding and repairing.</span><br>
<br>
<img src="https://docs.despical.dev/images/mazeengine/chat/chat-delete-review-clear.png" alt="Removal review showing the affected area and the clear-terrain policy."><br>
<span>Removal asks you to review the area and terrain policy before confirming.</span></div><br>
<br>
This example has <strong>no saved terrain snapshot</strong>, so removal clears the maze&#x27;s bounded volume. Restoration requires a snapshot captured before construction. Repair rewrites planned non-air blocks; it does not preserve additions placed where a wall or floor belongs.<br>

<hr><div align="center"><span><span><strong>06  /  CONNECT YOUR SERVER</strong></span></span><br>
<h2><strong>Useful on its own. Flexible alongside your tools.</strong></h2></div><br>
<br>
<h3><strong>Integrations</strong></h3><br>
Bring your building tools, region rules and server displays into the same workflow.<br>
<br>
<ul><li><strong>WorldEdit</strong>: fit a centered maze into your cuboid selection with <strong>--selection</strong>. Use <strong>--snapshot</strong> to capture the original terrain before construction and restore it when you remove the maze.<br>
</li><li><strong>FastAsyncWorldEdit (FAWE)</strong>: a WorldEdit-compatible alternative for the same selection and terrain snapshot features.<br>
</li><li><strong>WorldGuard</strong>: checks region build permissions before an operation and again while blocks are placed, removed or restored. Maze Engine&#x27;s own bypass permission does not override WorldGuard.<br>
</li><li><strong>PlaceholderAPI</strong>: publish maze totals, player ownership, themes, status and progress through any display plugin that supports PlaceholderAPI. The expansion registers automatically; no eCloud download is needed.<br>
</li><li><strong>Public developer API</strong>: query saved mazes and routes, submit operations, follow progress and cancellation, and react to completion or failure events from your own plugin.<br>
</li></ul>These integrations are optional. Ordinary maze creation, previews and route guides work without them.<br>
<a href="https://docs.despical.dev/maze-engine/integrations/"><strong>Read the integration guide</strong></a>  ·  <a href="https://docs.despical.dev/maze-engine/api/getting-started/"><strong>Explore the developer API</strong></a><br>
<br>
<h3><strong>Placeholders for your displays</strong></h3><br>
Show a server-wide maze count, a player&#x27;s own collection, or the theme and progress of the maze they are exploring. Put these expressions into your PlaceholderAPI-compatible scoreboard, hologram or menu.<br>
<br>
<strong>Server totals and ownership</strong><br>
<ul><li><strong>%mazeengine_count%</strong>: saved maze records.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_preset_count%</strong>: currently configured presets.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_active_operations%</strong>: active maze operations, including planning.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_owned_count%</strong>: saved mazes owned by the supplied player.<br>
</li></ul><strong>A display that follows the player</strong><br>
<ul><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_theme%</strong>: the theme of their current maze.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_status%</strong>: its saved status, such as Ready or Failed.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_progress%</strong>: operation progress as a number from 0 to 100.<br>
</li></ul><strong>A display for one particular maze</strong><br>
Use <strong>%mazeengine_maze_garden_theme%</strong>, <strong>%mazeengine_maze_garden_status%</strong> or <strong>%mazeengine_maze_garden_progress%</strong> to follow the saved maze named <strong>garden</strong>, regardless of where the viewer stands. Replace <strong>garden</strong> with your maze&#x27;s identifier.<br>
<br>
<details><summary><strong>All maze fields and placeholder examples</strong></summary><br>
Every field below works in both forms: <strong>%mazeengine_current_&lt;field&gt;%</strong> for the player&#x27;s current maze, and <strong>%mazeengine_maze_&lt;name&gt;_&lt;field&gt;%</strong> for a named maze.<br>
<br>
<ul><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_name%</strong>: maze identifier.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_theme%</strong>: saved preset&#x27;s display name.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_difficulty%</strong>: Easy, Medium or Hard theme label.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_seed%</strong>: deterministic generation seed.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_status%</strong>: saved record status.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_cells_width%</strong>: logical width in cells.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_cells_depth%</strong>: logical depth in cells.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_path_width%</strong>: corridor width in blocks.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_wall_thickness%</strong>: wall thickness in blocks.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_wall_height%</strong>: wall height above the floor.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_roof%</strong>: whether the maze has a roof: true or false.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_route_length%</strong>: entrance-to-exit cell transitions.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_dead_ends%</strong>: dead ends in the saved layout.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_progress%</strong>: active percentage; an idle Ready maze returns 100, other idle states return 0.<br>
</li><li><strong>%mazeengine_current_snapshot%</strong>: whether the record indicates an original-terrain snapshot: true or false.<br>
</li></ul>For example, <strong>%mazeengine_maze_garden_route_length%</strong> shows the route length of <strong>garden</strong>, while <strong>%mazeengine_current_route_length%</strong> shows it for the maze the player is inside.<br>
<br>
Values refresh every <strong>10 ticks</strong>. Current-maze placeholders return empty text outside a saved maze&#x27;s volume; named-maze placeholders return empty text when that maze is missing. Counts include saved records even when they are busy or failed.<br>
<br>
Test an expression in game with <strong>/papi parse me %mazeengine_count%</strong>.<br>
<a href="https://docs.despical.dev/maze-engine/placeholders/"><strong>Open the complete PlaceholderAPI reference</strong></a><br>
</details><br>

<hr><div align="center"><span><span><strong>07  /  BUILD YOUR FIRST MAZE</strong></span></span><br>
<h2><strong>Start with a theme. See where it takes you.</strong></h2></div><br>
<br>
<strong>Requirements:</strong> Paper for Minecraft <strong>1.21.11 or later</strong> and <strong>Java 25</strong>. Folia is not supported. WorldEdit or FAWE is needed only for selections and terrain snapshots.<br>
<br>
Download the JAR, place it in your server&#x27;s <strong>plugins</strong> folder and restart. Default settings and the 30 presets are installed automatically.<br>
<br>
<div align="center"><strong>/maze presets</strong><br>
Choose a theme.<br>
<br>
<strong>/maze preview garden 15 15 --preset hedge --seed 42</strong><br>
Inspect your idea.<br>
<br>
<strong>/maze create garden</strong><br>
Step outside the preview, then build it.<br>
<br>
<strong>/maze tp garden</strong><br>
Go explore. Use <strong>/maze solve garden</strong> if you need a guide.<br>
